Its a two pointer problem with one pointer iterating through linearly. The at the start of the for loop are important. First if there are duplicate numbers, they must be skipped lest we end up with duplicate triples in the result. Ex: [1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 5] should not go over the 2 in index 1 and the 2 in index 2. j is left pointer, k is right pointer. Calculate the toatl each time. If the total is positive, reduce the right pointer. Since nums is sorted, this decrements the total for future triples. Ex: [1, 2, 2, 3, 4] right pointer might go from 4 to 3. If positive increment the left pointer. If total is 0, meaning it is a triple, append it to results and increment the left pointer. The while loop below is to check for duplicates again.
